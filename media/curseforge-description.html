SkillGuide Forever answers one question fast:

“What can I train, at what level — and what am I still missing?”

Open a clean, Blizzard-styled window and browse class trainer skills or profession recipes. Class mode defaults to your current class; professions mode covers primary crafts plus Cooking, First Aid, and Fishing.

Why you’ll want it

  • Plan your next trainer trip — see what’s available now vs what unlocks later
  • Compare classes — great for alts, leveling advice, or “what does that class learn at 30?”
  • Profession recipes — learn skill, trainer vs recipe source, and O/Y/G/Gray breakpoints
  • Built for WoW Forever — data is seeded for Forever, not Classic Era leftovers
  • Zero clutter — dedicated windows, no spellbook takeover, no library stack

Features

  • Class skills window and separate professions window
  • Dropdowns for all 9 classes and primary + secondary professions
  • Lists with icon, name, rank/skill level, and train/learn requirements
  • Own-character cues: available, known, and locked
  • Hide known + search on both windows
  • Shift-click a row to link the spell/recipe into open chat
  • Movable windows that remember position

How to use

Class skills: /sg or /skillguideforever

Professions: /pg or /skillguideprofessions

Pick a list, search if you want, turn on Hide known while leveling, and head to the trainer with a clear checklist.

Notes

  • Targets WoW Forever (Interface 16001)
  • Pet abilities / grimoires are not listed
  • Gathering professions may have sparse Wowhead listview rows vs crafts
  • Seeds are based on Wowhead Forever ability + profession data and may be refreshed as beta patches land
