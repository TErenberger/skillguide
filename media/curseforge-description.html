SkillGuide Forever answers one question fast:

“What skills can I train, at what level — and what am I still missing?”

Open a clean, Blizzard-styled window and browse every class’s trainer skills with ranks and train levels. It defaults to your current class, and you can flip through Warrior, Paladin, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Shaman, Mage, Warlock, or Druid anytime.

Why you’ll want it

  • Plan your next trainer trip — see what’s available now vs what unlocks later
  • Compare classes — great for alts, leveling advice, or “what does that class learn at 30?”
  • Built for WoW Forever — skill data is seeded for Forever, not Classic Era leftovers
  • Zero clutter — one window, no spellbook takeover, no library stack

Features

  • Class dropdown (defaults to you)
  • Skill list with icon, name, rank, and train level
  • Own-class cues: available, known, and locked
  • Hide known toggle so you only see what’s left
  • Search by skill name, rank, or level
  • Movable window that remembers position

How to use

Type /sgf or /skillguideforever.

Pick a class, search if you want, turn on Hide known while leveling, and head to the trainer with a clear list.

Notes

  • Targets WoW Forever (Interface 16001)
  • Pet abilities / grimoires are not listed
  • Skill seed is based on Wowhead Forever ability data and may be refreshed as beta patches land
